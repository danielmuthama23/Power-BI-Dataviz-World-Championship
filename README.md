# Power BI Dataviz World Championship

A Round 1 submission for the **Investment Challenge** from the Power BI Dataviz World Championship. This project presents a brand review and investment strategy for **Pebble & Pine**, a family-vacation resort brand with 45 hotels.

## Project Summary

Although Pebble & Pine's revenue has grown 4.5% since 2022, operating margin declined from 32.2% to 28.2%, reducing operating profit by $29 million. The report identifies the brand's strengths, weaknesses, and the most promising investment opportunities.

The proposed strategy invests **$71.2 million** of the available **$145 million** budget across five initiatives in two implementation waves:

- Revenue management system
- Kids club upgrades at six resorts
- 40 Black Forest cabins
- 60-room Banff resort extension
- Muskoka indoor water park

In the expected case, the three growth initiatives add $21.8 million in annual revenue and $11.9 million in annual operating profit by 2032. This represents a 16.7% run-rate yield against the 8% hurdle and adds 100 family rooms.

## Report Pages

### Page 1 — Brand Review

Reviews current performance and identifies four priority opportunities:

- Reduce OTA room-revenue dependence
- Improve the weak November-to-March operating periods
- Address guest satisfaction declines
- Prioritize renovations at the oldest resorts

### Page 2 — Investment Strategy

Allocates capital across the proposed initiatives, with scenario analysis and the key risks and trade-offs:

- Wave 1: launch in January 2027
- Wave 2: launch in September 2027
- $73.8 million retained for future investment
- Payback occurs after 2032
- The $88.4 million new resort and $24 million renovation do not meet the hurdle
